Questions

Before you
start a track.

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

How long does an assessment take?

Most tracks run 12–24 hours of proctored work, split across a window of up to two weeks so you can pace it around a job or studies.

What happens if I don't pass?

You get a detailed rubric breakdown from your reviewer and can resit the assessment after 14 days at no extra cost.

How do employers verify a credential?

Every badge links to a public verification page showing the signed hash, issue date and rubric score — no login required.

Do credentials expire?

Most tracks stay valid for three years, after which you can recertify at a discounted rate to reflect current tooling.

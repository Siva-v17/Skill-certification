Questions

Before you
get started.

Can't find what you need here — reach out to the team.

How long does an assessment take?

+

Most tracks run 90–180 minutes, proctored in a single sitting. You'll see the exact duration before you start.

Who grades the attempts?

+

Working practitioners in that field, scoring against a public rubric — never an automated multiple-choice check.

Can employers verify a badge without an account?

+

Yes. Every public profile carries a verification link, and the API returns authenticity and grade in under 300ms.

Do custom tracks require a minimum team size?

+

No minimum, though most custom tracks make the most sense once you're hiring for a role every quarter or more.

What happens when a certification expires?

+

We send renewal reminders ahead of the expiry date, and your compliance dashboard flags anything lapsing soon.

About Stackly Certify

Built for people
who did the work.

We started Stackly Certify because a résumé line and a real skill are not the same thing — and hiring teams were tired of guessing which was which.

Our origin

From a hiring
problem to a protocol.

In 2023, three engineering leads kept rejecting candidates whose certificates didn't match their interview performance. So they built an assessment format that couldn't be faked: real tools, real constraints, real review by people still doing the job. Stackly Certify grew from that one internal rubric into a marketplace of 180 tracks across engineering, data, security, design and product.

The process

How a credential
actually gets earned.

Every track follows the same four stages, whether you're testing for a frontend role or a security audit. Nothing skips the queue.

01

Pick a track

Choose from 180 tracks scoped to a real job function, not a generic syllabus. Each track lists the tools, time limit and rubric up front.

02

Work the assessment

Timed, tool-based, and built to mirror the actual job — a debugging task, a design brief, a live data problem. No multiple choice.

03

Get graded by a practitioner

A working professional in that exact discipline reviews your submission against the published rubric, with written feedback either way.

04

Carry the credential anywhere

A verifiable badge with a public link to the graded work — embed it on a résumé, LinkedIn, or a portfolio, and any employer can check it.
